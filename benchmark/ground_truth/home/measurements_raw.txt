COZMO MEASURING SHEET 
Measure each thing TWICE; if the two differ by more than 0.5 cm, measure a third time.
Write both readings:  Wall A: 312.5 / 312.7

HOW TO LABEL WALLS
Stand in the doorway between the rooms, facing INTO the room.
The wall with that door is wall A. Go clockwise: B, C, D, ...
Every straight piece of wall gets its own letter, even short ones
(e.g. the 40 cm side of a cupboard bump or chimney).
Draw a quick sketch on paper with the letters and photograph it.
